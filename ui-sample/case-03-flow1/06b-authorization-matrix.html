Who can do what

Some rules belong to the whole workspace. The rest belong to each application separately — a person can hold a different role in each one. A ✓ means that role is allowed; the line underneath says whether it is also limited to certain records.

Workspace

Applies everywhere, whichever application you are in. These are not an application’s to decide, so no role can grant them.

Applications

Each application declares its own roles and decides what they may do inside it. A role means nothing outside the application that declared it.

HAPPENS ON ITS OWN
A document’s status follows its steps: it becomes Approved once every step approves, Rejected the moment one rejects, and goes back to In review otherwise. Nobody sets it by hand — not even an admin — so it is not something a role can be given.
